# phylloptim 0.27.1

- A stem route evaluated at a prescribed stem potential (`evaluate_psi_stem_at()`) now reports that point's `lambda_emergent`; it reported the previous solve's. No other number moves.
- `leaf_gradient_batch()` refuses a price its model does not read, with `leaf_solve()`'s message, whether it came from `leaf_batch()` or a supplied `theta`, and a missing `TF24_floor_lambda_o` before solving rather than as per-row errors.
- `leaf_solve()` refuses a price that is not a single number; it seats one price for every row.
- `leaf_batch()` accepts a price that varies by observation while `leaf_specific_conductance_max` and the resistance are shared; it failed building `theta`.
- Plan section A, [#207](https://github.com/traitecoevo/phylloptim/issues/207).

# phylloptim 0.24.0 – 0.27.0

- **0.27.0**: on a curve `psi_crit` does not bound, the collar solve decides a dry pin at the end of the collar interval rather than a step inside it, so pinned and interior answers meet continuously; a few near-end pins become interior (profit moves by ~1e-9 relative), plant's seasonal stands run 2% slower, and plant must recapture its reference gradients (its drought and seasonal sweeps move by about 1%) ([#205](https://github.com/traitecoevo/phylloptim/pull/205)).
- **0.26.2**: `ci` is solved by safeguarded Newton rather than TOMS748: the collar solve is 21% faster, the stem routes 40-47%, and plant's TF24 stands 22%; values move by up to ~1e-13 relative ([#203](https://github.com/traitecoevo/phylloptim/pull/203)).
- **0.26.0 – 0.26.1**: SOX and JW26 shut a leaf that cannot fix carbon (`A <= 0` at every opening, as in the dark) at the wet bound with no flow, where the stem route pinned it at `psi_crit` and the collar route refused it; rows with `A > 0` anywhere do not move. Separately, a shut leaf whose roots outlast its stem has its stem seated at its collar on the C++ derivative surface, so its root-trait profit rows read ~0 instead of up to 9e-3; nothing the R layer returns moves ([#201](https://github.com/traitecoevo/phylloptim/pull/201), [#202](https://github.com/traitecoevo/phylloptim/pull/202)).
- **0.25.0**: the vulnerability curves' tail is carried as `G(inf) - G`, so flux and conductivity keep their precision to the end of the curve: a leaf no longer shuts at ~12.4 MPa for want of digits, a root layer conducts to the end of its curve (15.4 MPa at the defaults, was 14.5), and `Leaf::stem_capacity_floor` is removed ([#199](https://github.com/traitecoevo/phylloptim/pull/199)).
- **0.24.0**: with the energy balance on, the CO2 conductance treats the boundary layer as diffusing at `r_s^(2/3)` rather than the stomatal 1.67, so `A` rises by a median 0.3-0.8% (at most 1.4%) and `gc` by up to 4.1%; `gs_H2O` is still the total conductance to water. Off the energy balance nothing moves ([#197](https://github.com/traitecoevo/phylloptim/pull/197)).

- **0.21.0**: the vulnerability integral `G` is a C2 quintic table (error 2e-15 of `G(inf)` at the defaults, was up to 8.7e-11), so a P50 change rebuilds nothing; values move at that level, and further where the old `psi_crit` bound had pinned a leaf; the collar solve is 1-4% slower (plant's TF24 stands about 11%) ([#188](https://github.com/traitecoevo/phylloptim/pull/188)).
